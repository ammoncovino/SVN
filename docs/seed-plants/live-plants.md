## PLANT 11 — Biosphere
### Big money buys access, not just ads. Here's a fix that's been tested.

74% of Americans call money in politics a very big problem. Democrats and Republicans both. It's the one thing we agree on.

Here's what gets me. In one experiment, congressional offices gave meetings to people identified as donors about five times as often as to regular constituents: 12.5% of the time versus 2.4%.

A fix that's been tried: Seattle gives every registered voter four $25 vouchers to give to any city candidate they choose. The number of people giving to campaigns went up 350%, and more people ran for office.

Why it helps: candidates can raise money from regular people instead of chasing a few big checks.

The catch: it's paid for with public money, and it doesn't touch super PACs.

Would you take $100 in vouchers to back the candidates you choose?
